Red Flag 1 — "No true OOS test"
--------------------------------
This was done. Here's what actually happened:

v7 optimization was performed on Year 1 data only (Feb 2025 - Feb
2026). A formal walk-forward test was run:

  In-sample (Feb-Aug 2025):      PF 1.597, 102 trades, 49.0% WR
  Out-of-sample (Aug 25-Feb 26): PF 1.224, 72 trades, 44.4% WR
  PF ratio (OOS/IS):             0.77 (above the 0.7 robustness threshold)

v8 then added two structural changes (short-only + percentage-based
stops) and was validated on the full 2-year dataset (Feb 2024 - Feb
2026). Year 2 (Feb 2024 - Feb 2025) was never seen during any
optimization — it is completely out-of-sample.

Year 2 result: +$3,412, PF 1.129, 87 trades — profitable on data
the strategy never trained on.

Full 2-year statistical validation:
  t-test p-value:         0.028
  Permutation p-value:    0.013 (10,000 permutations)
  Bootstrap P(profit):    98.75%
  95% CI avg trade:       $18.67 to $321.27 (entirely positive)

The OOS gate is met. We just didn't put the walk-forward numbers in
the script header.


Red Flag 2 — "High parameter count (~15+)"
-------------------------------------------
The code has ~15 parameters, but the count is misleading. Most are
OFF or insensitive:

  VA Fade:    OFF by default — 0 tuned parameters
  80% Rule:   OFF by default — 0 tuned parameters
  TEMA slow:  Tested 16-28, insensitive (no meaningful change)
  TEMA trend: Tested 39-69, insensitive (no meaningful change)
  TEMA fast:  9 is optimal, but 5 and 13 both produce similar results

The actual decisions that shaped v8 are:

  1. Short-only (binary — on/off)
  2. 30bps percentage stop (one parameter)
  3. Skip Friday (binary — on/off)
  4. Noon blackout 12:00-13:00 (binary — on/off)

That's 4 decisions, not 15. Each was A/B tested independently on
the full dataset, not jointly optimized. The A/B results:

  Skip Fridays:   Removed 50 trades at 30% WR, net +$13,415
  Noon blackout:  Removed 25 trades at 33.3% WR, net +$10,320
  Short-only:     Removed longs losing -$5,589 (PF 0.912)
  30bps stop:     Replaced fixed 20pt stop that didn't scale with price

Sensitivity analysis on TEMA lengths was done in v5.1 (22-test
parameter sweep across 4 rounds). The strategy is robust to wide
parameter ranges — that's evidence against overfitting.


Red Flag 3 — "Short-only in a bull market"
-------------------------------------------
Fair question, and we investigated it directly. Longs don't fail
because of the bull market — they fail because IB breakdowns are
inherently bearish signals.

Breaking below the Initial Balance low with bearish TEMA slope is
a short setup by nature. The pattern itself is asymmetric. Flipping
it long (buying breakouts above IB high with bullish TEMA) produced:

  IB SHORT: +$8,369 over 2 years
  IB LONG:  -$1,830 over 2 years

Even in bull markets there are plenty of strong down-trend days.
The IB breakdown catches those. Trying to force the same logic in
reverse simply doesn't produce edge — the pattern doesn't work
symmetrically.

This is not optimization bias. It's a structural property of the
setup: auction theory breakdowns below the opening range are
directionally bearish events.


Red Flag 4 — "VA calculation is approximate"
---------------------------------------------
Correct, and we agree. VWAP +/- 1 stdev is a statistical proxy for
the true Market Profile Value Area (70% of volume). That's exactly
why VA Fade is OFF by default. It's on our roadmap to replace the
VWAP proxy with volume.profile_session() for accurate VA edges.
No argument on this point.


Red Flag 5 — "Low trade frequency"
------------------------------------
Fair concern, but trade frequency was never the goal. Win rate and
edge quality were.

We exhaustively tested every path to more trades:

  Cooldown 2 to 0:     No effect (signal is one-shot by design)
  Min IB range 8 to 3: +4 trades, negligible impact
  Max IB trades 2 to 4:+5 trades, diluted PF
  TEMA trend OFF:       +8 trades, nearly neutral
  VA Fade shorts:       +24 trades, but 29% WR and PF 0.54 — losers
  Drop noon blackout:   +25 trades, kills statistical significance
  Allow Fridays:        +50 trades, but 36% WR and PF 0.81 — losers

Every attempt to increase frequency degraded the edge. 177 trades
over 2 years is the natural frequency of high-quality IB breakdowns.
Adding trades just to increase sample size means adding losing trades.

More trades require a different strategy entirely, not looser
parameters on this one. That's why we built three additional
strategies for the portfolio.


Red Flag 6 — "Single direction, single setup"
----------------------------------------------
Correct observation, and it's intentional. We tested three setups:

  IB Breakout:     +$30,084 (PF 1.511) — KEPT
  TEMA Crossover:  -$3,313 (PF 0.788)  — REMOVED
  VA Fade:         Approximate VA, inconsistent — DISABLED

Turning off what doesn't work is discipline, not weakness. The code
infrastructure exists because we rigorously tested everything and
documented why each piece was kept or removed. The version history
from v1 through v8 shows each decision and its data.
